# Customization

Open WebUI works out of the box—but it really shines when you make it yours.

This section covers the features that transform a basic chat interface into something that feels native: web search for current information, voice for hands-free conversations, image generation for creative work, and model presets that remember exactly how you like things configured.

By the end, you'll have an AI assistant that rivals (or exceeds) what you'd get from commercial alternatives—running on your terms.

---

## What's in This Section

### 🔍 [Adding Web Search](./customization/adding-web-search)
Give your AI access to current information. News, documentation, recent events—anything beyond its training data.

### ⚙️ [Creating Your First Model Preset](./customization/first-model-preset)
Save your preferred settings—system prompts, parameters, defaults—so every conversation starts exactly how you want.

### 🎙️ [Setting Up Voice](./customization/setting-up-voice)
Talk to your AI and hear it respond. Configure speech-to-text and text-to-speech for a conversational experience.

### 🎨 [Setting Up Image Generation](./customization/image-generation)
Generate images from text descriptions. Connect a local backend like ComfyUI or use cloud APIs.

---

## The Goal

When you're done with this section, your Open WebUI instance will:

- **Search the web** when you need current information
- **Listen and speak** for hands-free interaction
- **Generate images** from your descriptions
- **Remember your preferences** across sessions

You're not adapting to your tools anymore. You're adapting your tools to your specific needs.
