# Remote Access

You built something powerful. Now use it from anywhere.

One of the biggest advantages of self-hosted AI is that it's *yours*—but that shouldn't mean it's trapped on your local network. With the right setup, your Open WebUI instance becomes accessible from any device, anywhere, while staying completely under your control.

No cloud provider in the middle. No subscription fees. No data leaving your infrastructure unless you want it to.

## Why This Matters

Most "AI assistants" require you to send every conversation through someone else's servers. That's the tradeoff for convenience—you get access anywhere, but you give up control everywhere.

Self-hosted flips that equation. Your AI runs on your hardware, your data stays local, and your conversations remain private [4]. The only missing piece is *access*.

Tailscale solves this without the complexity of traditional VPNs or the risk of exposing services to the public internet. It creates an encrypted mesh network between your devices—your laptop, your phone, your server—all appearing on the same private network regardless of where they physically are.

Add the PWA on top, and you've got something that feels indistinguishable from a native app. Launch from your home screen, talk to your AI, get answers—whether you're at home, at work, or somewhere without reliable connectivity.

**The result:** ChatGPT-level convenience with none of the privacy compromises.
